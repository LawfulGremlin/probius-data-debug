## Balance Update

### Heroes

#### Chromie

  - **Deep Breathing**

#### Gazlowe

##### Base

- Health increased from 2275 to 2375.
- **Deth Lazor \[W\]**

- **Level 20**
  - **Bomb Toss**

##### Talents

- **Level 7**
  - **Incendiary Elixir**

##### Talents

#### Medivh

##### Base

  - **Marked Resilience**
    - Shield amount increased from 160 to 200.
  - **Relentless Whispers**

  - **Fractured Reality**
    - Damage bonus reduced from 25% to 15%.
- **Level 20**
  - **Harbinger's Consumption**

  - **Unstable Core**
    - No longer applies a damage over time to targets.
    - Impact damage increased from 45 to 75.
      - 🟢 **Probius addition — not in official notes:** The fire damage value of 45 and duration value of 5 were removed. (interpreted from game data)
